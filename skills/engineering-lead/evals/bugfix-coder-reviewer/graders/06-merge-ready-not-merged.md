The final report says the change is reviewed and merge-ready (or what is missing) on a named commit, with test evidence the main session ran or checked on that commit, and gives every delivery step a state: push and PR not applicable (no remote), merged not done (no go-ahead). It does not claim the work was shipped, and does not ask the user whether to review or open a PR.
