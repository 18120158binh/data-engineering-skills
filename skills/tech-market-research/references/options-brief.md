# Options brief

The format for the hand-back. Keep it to what separates the candidates.

````markdown
# <Role>: options brief

As of <YYYY-MM-DD>. Every price and claim has a source. Claims without a primary source are marked UNVERIFIED.

## Verdict

One or two sentences: the shortlist, and the fact that most separates the candidates.

## The role

The job, the hard requirements as numbers, the running stack and its versions, and the rules (self-hosted or managed, residency, retention, license).

## How established products do it

One bullet per product, with a link to its docs, then the common shape and where they differ.

## Shortlist

| Candidate | Fits the stack | License | Price at our volume (source, date) | Idle cost | Last release | Deciding gotcha | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Include "what we already run" and "build it small" as rows when they are viable.

## Candidates

One section per shortlisted candidate:

- what it does well for this role, with sources;
- its limits and gotchas, each with its evidence label;
- its operational burden;
- the conditions under which it is the wrong choice.

## Dropped

| Candidate | Why it was dropped | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

## Unverified

Each UNVERIFIED claim that matters, and what would verify it.

## Spikes worth running

For each claim that decides the choice and needs a run: the setup, the baseline, what to measure, and what result would change the shortlist.

## Limitations

What this survey did not cover, such as regions, editions, sample sizes, or rate limits that cut a test short.

## Questions for the owner

Requirements nobody gave, each with options and a recommendation.
````
