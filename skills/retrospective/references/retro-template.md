# Retrospective template

Save the retrospective as `retros/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`, or wherever the repo keeps them. Add one dated line to the repo's retrospective log.

```markdown
# Retro: <what was looked back on>, <YYYY-MM-DD>

## Scope

What the work set out to do, its definition of done, and the time range or pull requests covered.

## Facts

- Shipped: <what>
- Reversals: <each, with its commit or record>
- Rework: <review rounds per change, work done twice>
- Caught late: <each defect, who found it, and the check that should have>
- Waits: <time blocked, and on what>

## Decisions

| Decision | Predicted | Happened | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|

Verdict is one of: held, revisit, or too early to tell.

## Went well

- <what to keep doing, with evidence>

## Problems and causes

| Problem | Evidence | Cause | Change | File | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Last retro's actions

| Action | Helped? | Keep, adjust, or revert |
|---|---|---|

## Dropped

One-offs and findings that need no change, each with the reason.

## For the owner

Questions and pending decisions only the owner can settle.
```
